The Journey

How It Works

01

Consultation

Send your reference photo and desired size via the contact form. We'll agree on the composition and deadline, and I'll send a final quote.

02

Deposit & Sketch

A deposit confirms your spot in the calendar. I prepare an initial sketch layout for your review before beginning the final piece.

03

Drawing & Polish

I build layers of the chosen medium, sending you a mid-way progress check to ensure the likeness and mood are exactly right.

04

Delivery

Treated with protective fixative, packaged safely flat or rolled, and delivered to your door with tracking (or digital scan).

A Note on Reference Photos

For realistic portraits, clear reference photos taken in natural daylight (avoiding heavy direct flash) produce the best results. If you have several photos and aren't sure which works best, feel free to share a few and I can help you select the ideal one.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the turnaround time?

Typically 4 to 6 weeks depending on size, detail, and current queue. If you have a specific deadline (such as a birthday, wedding, or anniversary), please mention it upfront and I will do my best to accommodate your schedule.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, artwork is safely shipped worldwide from Dublin, Ireland. Pieces are packed flat between sturdy rigid backing boards or in heavy-duty moisture-resistant postal tubes, fully tracked until delivery.

Can you combine separate reference photos?

Yes! If you have loved ones, pets, or subjects that were never photographed together, I can compose and balance them into a single harmonious and cohesive drawing.

How do payments and revisions work?

A deposit confirms your spot in the calendar. I share an initial composition sketch for approval before starting final shading. During the process, 2 revision stages are included so you can share feedback and feel completely confident in the final piece.

Looking to support a charity?

You can also commission a ballpoint pen piece where 100% of proceeds go directly to the Meningitis Research Foundation for my Kilimanjaro climb. See the Fundraise page for details.
