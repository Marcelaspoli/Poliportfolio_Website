🎨 Marcela Poli: Art Portfolio Website

A hand-coded art portfolio website built from scratch with HTML and CSS only.
It showcases my drawings and paintings, lets people get in touch and make commissions, and supports my charity fundraising for the Meningitis Research Foundation.

Live site: https://marcelaspoli.github.io/Poliportfolio_Website/commissions.html

✨ Pages
Page	File	What it's for
Portfolio	index.html	A gallery of my past artworks
About Me	about.html	Who I am, my story and my creative process
Contact	contact.html	Contact me (sends the information directly to my email)
Commissions commissions.html Explore your options for commission 
Fundraising	fundraise.html	My Kilimanjaro 2027 challenge for the Meningitis Research Foundation and commission to donate

Every page shares the same header (my handwritten signature as the logo, with my name underneath), a navigation bar, and a footer with an Instagram link.

🏔️ About the Fundraising Page

In August 2027 I'm taking on the Kilimanjaro Student Challenge in aid of the Meningitis Research Foundation, with a fundraising goal of €5,590, supporting the charity's aim of defeating meningitis by 2030.

The fundraising page explains why I'm doing it, how the money will be used, and how to donate or support through art-based fundraising.

🛠️ Built With
HTML5: semantic markup for every page
CSS3: Flexbox and CSS Grid for layout, custom properties for colours and fonts
Responsive design: media queries so the site works on phones, tablets and desktops
Git & GitHub: version control and hosting

No JavaScript frameworks or external libraries.
